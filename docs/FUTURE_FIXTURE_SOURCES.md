# Future-fixture source assessment (23 September 2026)

**Implemented:** [OpenFootball's 2026/27 JSON files](https://github.com/openfootball/football.json/tree/master/2026-27) are a guarded, cached *schedule* source for E0, SP1, D1, I1, F1, N1 and P1. Completed results from Football-Data.co.uk remain authoritative, the weekly CSV remains a fallback, and Data Quality shows source/coverage per league. There is no verified free, full-season, current source covering all ten leagues and the domestic cups without a provider account.

## Integration guardrails

1. Map the seven file codes explicitly to app leagues and reconcile names to current-season club IDs. Keep provider name/ID aliases separate from the app's canonical club keys; examples include `Arsenal FC` versus `Arsenal`, `AFC Bournemouth` versus `Bournemouth` in the [English file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openfootball/football.json/master/2026-27/en.1.json).
2. Accept only unscored future fixtures with valid dates and known clubs. Before replacing one source's partition, check the expected league size, 380/306 ordered home-away pair count, duplicate pairs, unknown teams and future-row count; reject an HTML/404 response, incomplete or stale file, or a suspicious schedule regression. Preserve last good data and report source freshness.
3. Prefer completed Football-Data.co.uk results over schedule rows. Match by league, season and canonical ordered club pair; rescheduled fixtures must update rather than duplicate when the date changes. Keep raw source date/time, fetch timestamp and source hash for audit; `time` is nullable/provisional.
4. Cache the seven small, mutable-at-the-same-URL snapshots for a bounded period (e.g. 6–24 hours) with conditional GET if supported. A 05:00 UTC JSON regeneration is **not** a guarantee of an upstream change; avoid per-page network calls. Show coverage and freshness in Settings/Data Quality.
5. Domestic cups are **not solved** by this source. The [2026/27 England upstream directory](https://github.com/openfootball/england/tree/master/2026-27) currently lists league and Championship files, while [Spain](https://github.com/openfootball/espana/tree/master/2026-27) lists its league file; neither lists a current domestic cup calendar. Cup brackets cannot be made into complete match lists before draws. Treat official cup announcements or a licensed provider as competition-specific work, never synthesize fixtures.

The source is better than the current weekly CSV for seven league calendars and has clearer redistribution rights. Its volunteer update lag, sparse kickoff times and three-league gap mean it should be a complementary adapter, not a replacement for the existing guarded source or factual match-result pipeline.
