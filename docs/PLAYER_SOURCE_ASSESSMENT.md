# Player-source assessment — 10 September 2026

No evaluated source passes the gate for player goals, assists, minutes and age across all 12 selected clubs and five completed seasons. Complete player-season analytics therefore remain unavailable. Iteration 2.2 enables a separate, visibly capped player explorer using TheSportsDB: up to 10 profiles per elite club and up to 10 historical statistic rows per player. Missing players, seasons and values remain missing.

| Source | Free access | Coverage result | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| football-data.org | Free tier | Historical seasons and deep player data require paid packages | Reject |
| TheSportsDB v1 | Free key, 30 calls/minute | Free player and statistic responses are capped and do not provide complete historical rosters | Accept for labelled profile sample; reject for complete analytics |
| StatsBomb Open Data | Free repository | Selected competitions only | Reject |
| Understat | Public website | No supported public API or clear automated reuse permission verified | Reject |
